# Lessons — accumulated mission experience

Read whole at Step 0 of every mission. Newest entries last. Entries are appended by the Step-7 retrospective; behavior-changing lessons get folded into SKILL.md/references with user approval and compressed here to pointers.

## 2026-07-25 — extractor-speedup (kriswill/flake-explorer, 2 teammates, 6 green PRs, 1.7-3.1x) — founding mission

The mission this skill was distilled from; most of its lessons ARE the skill (briefs, msg-teammate.sh, heavy.lock, validator role, negative controls, arithmetic-gated designs, auto-merge). Recorded here beyond what the skill already encodes:

- LESSON: A wrong fact in a brief costs the teammate's first work block; label beliefs vs verified facts, and verify anything cheap to verify (one guessed "the crate is sync" was wrong; one assignment referenced an env var that existed only on an unmerged branch).
  Action: encoded in mission-prompt.md preamble; re-check assignments against *main*, not against a teammate's branch you happen to remember.
- LESSON: Validators produce impeccable measurements and fallible interpretations — the duplication blocker's measurement was exact while its root-cause theory would have lost data. Adjudicate by artifact citation, give the code's author right of reply, and be ready to withdraw your own directives fast.
  Action: encoded in SKILL.md Step 5; kept here because the failure mode recurs in new shapes.
- LESSON: Structural bounds beat samplers for detecting broken attribution (17 "concurrent" processes under an 8-wide semaphore was the contamination tell).
  Action: encoded in protocols.md; generalize: when a measurement violates an invariant, distrust the measurement first.
- LESSON: Wins are path-dependent — the same change measured 17% (isolated path) and 2.8% (saturated path) with opposite memory signs. Require the trade-off table to show every user-relevant path before any headline number is quoted.
  Action: encoded in brief template (PR section).
- LESSON: Teammate sessions strand queued self-prompts in their input box when their turn ends; an idle-with-queued-input watchdog would have saved ~6 manual interventions.
  Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — scripts/heartbeat.sh watches for idle-with-queued-input (2-read debounce) and auto-submits via msg-teammate.sh's verified bare-Enter; msg-teammate.sh's bare-Enter mode remains the manual tool.
- LESSON: Fresh flakes' nix eval caches confound first-ever timings by ~5x; "cold" must be defined (cold data dir vs cold machine) beside every number.
  Action: encoded in brief measurement regime.

## 2026-07-27 — wowdps-team-build (kriswill/wowdps, 3 teammates, shipped TUI meter; 238 tests; 0 unparsed across 493k real lines; 3 self-caught wrong claims)

New-project greenfield mission (no CI, local merges only, no PRs) with a live external data source (the user's active WoW raid) as the real target. Herdr multiplexer path exercised end-to-end for the first time: spawn/msg/agent-list all worked, zero delivery failures, zero queued-prompt rescues. (Reconciled 2026-07-28: this mission's integration mode is now declarable as policy `local-merge` — no GitHub, coordinator merges locally — added to the integration-policy gate for exactly this shape.)

- LESSON: Get the real artifact flowing on day zero, before implementers design from documentation. Ground truth refuted the canonical-looking wiki (would have shifted every value 2 columns), corrected the better spec once, and corrected the validator's own fixture 4 times; six binding parser corrections came from real lines that no doc mentioned.
  Evidence: journal 21:38–23:10 — every offset dispute settled by the live log within minutes of it existing. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — SKILL.md Step 2 gained the "Real input" recon item (enable real data before implementers design from documentation).
- LESSON: Label unverified technical claims in briefs as "hypothesis — verify before building", never "directive". The coordinator's parse-from-end strategy was wrong; the word "directive" invites compliance over verification, and only the mandatory design-review step made the refutation free.
  Evidence: core's retro #3; design post 20:50 refuted the directive with spec evidence before any code. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — brief template preamble mandates HYPOTHESIS labeling for unverified claims, never "directive".
- LESSON: Commission duplicate derivation deliberately when a data format/protocol is load-bearing: two teammates independently deriving the same field table (and agreeing) is what made "the reference is wrong" trustworthy. It happened by accident this mission.
  Evidence: core and validator independently found spec.json and the 19-field block within the same hour. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — brief template gained an optional DATA-FORMAT MISSIONS duplicate-derivation line.
- LESSON: Require implementers to echo back the full interface surface they depend on (keymap, public fns, derives) in their design post. The echo caught a coordinator contract bug (one key bound twice) before code existed, and tui's derive-requirements note pre-solved integration.
  Evidence: tui design 13:05; tui retro #4. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — design posts must echo the depended-on interface surface (brief template engineering discipline).
- LESSON: Stubs standing in for a peer's module must mimic the real construction contract (start EMPTY, not pre-seeded with demo data); and any producer/consumer hazard needs its check on BOTH sides — mitigating chunked reads producer-side did not stop an unbounded consumer drain from freezing the UI for 10s on a 154MB input.
  Evidence: 19 predicted test failures at swap, all from stub pre-seeding; drain-starvation bug found only by the live-log run. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — brief template discipline bullet: large-input smoke at milestone 1, both-sides hazard checks, stubs start EMPTY.
- LESSON: Pre-measure cross-teammate integrations on the owning branch before handing them over: core compiled tui's swap on its own branch and reported "0 compile errors, 19 test failures, root cause X", turning integration into one mechanical round.
  Evidence: core status 21:17; tui completed the swap + adaptations in a single milestone. Action: recorded; generalize as "the teammate whose module arrives second pre-measures the join".
- LESSON: Golden files are only trustworthy if regeneration is gated on a diff against the committed copy — the validator's checker silently lost ALL pet attribution (uninitialised awk var) and the only thing that caught it was diff-before-regenerate. Corollary: design expected values around the RULE (contract), not around what currently passes; the R7 gating flip then costs zero recomputation.
  Evidence: validator retro #4 and #5. Action: recorded.
- LESSON: In event-stream aggregation domains, hunt duplicate-event families FIRST (the same fact reported twice under different names): SWING_DAMAGE_LANDED and *_SUPPORT would each have silently inflated every number, and no test catches a double-count you don't know exists. Empirical dedup proof = summed totals per source identical across the twin streams.
  Evidence: rulings R1/R3; validator's per-source equality check 21:38. Action: recorded.
- LESSON: TUI smoke-testing needs a real terminal emulator: script(1) cannot host crossterm apps (no answer to the cursor-position query). A detached `tmux -L <sock>` server inside the agent's own pane + capture-pane assertions works headless, opens no window, and can assert exact rendered numbers.
  Evidence: tui's discovery at milestone 1; validator's screen-level verification of hand-computed values. Action: recorded for any TUI mission.
- LESSON: A "verified vs synthetic-only" scoreboard in the final verdict (which behaviors real data exercised, which only fixtures did) keeps a green suite honest — this mission ships with three shapes gated only synthetically (dispels, _SUPPORT, off-hand swing) and says so.
  Evidence: validator stand-down 23:42. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — SKILL.md Step 6 requires the verified-vs-synthetic split in the report.

## 2026-07-28 — multiplexer-findings-fix (kriswill/dotfiles, 2 teammates, 15/15 findings fixed, 17/17 e2e controls, GREEN merge)

- LESSON: `herdr agent start` spawns from the SERVER's env, not the caller's — a coordinator with a custom CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR strands teammates on an OAuth login screen.
  Evidence: first spawn landed on a login prompt; --env forward fixed it. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — kept with user approval (commit 88dbc79); spawn-teammate.sh forwards CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR via --env.
- LESSON: Mark every brief recipe as HYPOTHESIS unless verified — both validator rigs lost time to brief "facts" that were wrong (the tier-a socket knob names, "server spawns commands without pane env vars").
  Evidence: e2e retro #1; the HERDR_SOCKET_PATH knob is honored by the client, not the server. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — brief template preamble covers HYPOTHESIS labeling for all claims and recipes.
- LESSON: Mandate a POSITIVE self-check per rig from the start, not just negative controls: "a test that FAILS on both arms may also prove nothing" — a mis-wired rig is indistinguishable from the defect it hunts.
  Evidence: t01 false-FAIL and a t12 re-run 0/3 both traced to a stacked nested client consuming the chords; the negative-control-only protocol accepted them until the self-check rule was invented mid-mission. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — validator role text: every rig ships a rehearsed positive control; a broken rig reports ERROR, never FAIL.
- LESSON: A probe must SURVIVE and NOT REPLACE what it measures. Two shapes this mission: an instrumented binding that replaced the forward action under test, and a delivery probe killed by the very byte it observed (0x1C = tty SIGQUIT kills any non-raw-mode observer, mimicking "chord swallowed").
  Evidence: t16 mis-diagnosis chain; impl's cat -v probe died to SIGQUIT. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — brief template discipline bullet: probe survives and doesn't replace what it observes; assert the artifact's path/pid inside the probe.
- LESSON: Verify a diagnosis AT THE LAYER IT CLAIMS before scoping work from it — binding-table, injection, and receipt are different layers; measuring one and generalising caused the only wasted scope addition (a "fix" that was really inert-config cleanup).
  Evidence: impl's list-keys + raw-pty artifacts vs e2e's receipt-layer observation; the version-pair binds were inert, nothing was ever swallowed. Action: recorded; adjudication rule stays "specify the decisive experiment at the disputed layer" — it resolved both disputes this mission in <30 min each.
- LESSON: Decisive-experiment adjudication beats theory-ruling: both teammate disputes (env-id vs --current race; tmux over-escape) ended with the challenged party running a specified experiment and conceding on artifacts, no coordinator fiat needed.
  Evidence: t12 rapid-fire experiment (ids [p4 p4 p4] both variants); t16 uninstrumented re-run. Action: recorded — keep as the default dispute protocol.
- LESSON: Heartbeat "idle" races turn boundaries — a teammate can read idle in the gap after a delivered message before its turn starts; check the pane before treating it as a stall.
  Evidence: impl read idle 60s after a verified-delivery review directive, pane showed an active turn. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — scripts/heartbeat.sh debounces every non-working state to 2 consecutive reads.
- LESSON: Teammate self-stamped HH:MM in status lines drifts badly (impl's stamps ran ~2h behind wall clock); the stream's arrival order is the truth, not the stamps.
  Evidence: "15:25 DONE" arrived after "17:50" validator entries. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — scripts/log-status.sh stamps time and HEAD SHA itself; the brief template mandates it for every status line.
- LESSON: Validators need an explicit RETRACTION convention — a withdrawn claim must be as loud as the claim, and both self-caught retractions this mission had to invent their format.
  Evidence: e2e retro #3; two retractions (t01/t12 unsound, t16 root cause). Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — log-status.sh --retract emits the *** RETRACTION *** banner; brief template's status protocol names it.

## 2026-07-28 — keycloak-e2e-mission (PerforceCTO/p4c-portal, 3 teammates incl. mid-mission adversary, 5 PRs merged, e2e 9/1F->32/32, 2 security fixes shipped)

- LESSON: Never let a brief's "default stance" name the expected conclusion ("update tests to match the new code"). The failing test WAS the vulnerability firing; a teammate taking the cheap path would have agreed with the brief. Briefs state questions and required investigations, not expected answers — the mandatory hazard-investigation clause is what saved this one.
  Evidence: impl retro 2/3; null===null gate, rows 7/9. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — brief template preamble: questions and required investigations, never expected conclusions.
- LESSON: Claims of the form ALL/ONLY/LAST (audit completeness) are a distinct assertion class: require the enumeration command + pasted output before publishing. Both of impl's misses were this shape; both were adversary-caught and cheap to check.
  Evidence: "4 call sites/1 write" was 5/2; "tests verified load-bearing" was vacuous. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — brief template discipline bullet: ALL/ONLY/LAST claims publish only with the enumeration command + pasted output.
- LESSON: A guard test merges only with a red-run under the exact mutation it guards. Three vacuous-guard cases in one mission (dead conjunct 59/0; console-only capture green with full PII leak 28/0; static-call evasion) — all read as correct, all fell only to mutation.
  Evidence: A-1, R-4, R-6. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — brief template discipline bullet: guard tests merge only with a red run under the exact mutation they guard.
- LESSON: Declare the dependencies SHARED between teammate streams in every brief, and instruct reviewers to treat cross-stream assumption contradictions as a first-class finding class — the seam held the mission's biggest findings (impl assumed non-null session org normal while e2e was proving the mapper absent).
  Evidence: A-8; R-1's severity came from env-x-design cross-reference; adversary retro 1-2/3. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — brief template charter gained a Shared-dependencies line; cross-stream contradictions are first-class findings.
- LESSON: A dedicated adversarial-reviewer teammate (review-only, no git-write authority, mutation-mandated verdicts, MERGE-OK gate per PR) is worth its cost on security-adjacent missions.
  Evidence: 19 findings; every vacuous guard and the policy fork (A-2) came from it, none from CI or authors. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — SKILL.md Step 3 defines the optional adversarial-reviewer role (mutation-backed verdicts, MERGE-OK gate, report refutation).
- LESSON: herdr `tab create` lands tabs in the currently-FOCUSED workspace, and herdr has no tab-move — spawn MUST pass --workspace <coordinator's workspace, from HERDR_PANE_ID> or teammates scatter.
  Evidence: impl/e2e landed in wE (coordinator in wD); user requested a move that proved impossible; adversary later landed in wD purely by focus timing. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 (user-directed) — spawn-teammate.sh pins herdr tabs via --workspace "${HERDR_PANE_ID%%:*}" and tmux windows via -t "<session_id from TMUX_PANE>:", addressing the new window by window_id thereafter; protocols.md Spawning updated.
- LESSON: In herdr, identify agents by tab `label` via `herdr tab list` (agent list rows carry no teammate name), and debounce heartbeat idle/done alerts to 2+ consecutive reads — every single-read alert this mission was a turn-boundary race; also expect a third status "done" besides working/idle.
  Evidence: heartbeat v1 false-MISSING on a nonexistent JSON field; ~6 false idle/done alerts before v4 debounce. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — scripts/heartbeat.sh reads `herdr agent get <name>`'s agent_status (verified field), treats any non-working value as idle-class and reports it verbatim, and debounces to 2 reads; protocols.md updated.
- LESSON: Identifiers (SHAs, IDs) in status lines and PR bodies must be pasted from command output, never recalled — same rule class as timestamps-from-date.
  Evidence: impl cited commit 1c7be3f, an object that does not exist; real head 2e3503f. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — log-status.sh stamps the HEAD SHA; brief template's status protocol extends the paste-don't-recall rule to PR bodies.
- LESSON: When two identities behave differently against the same API, diff the identities first (roles, claims — one read call) before theorizing about state (caching, staleness, restarts).
  Evidence: e2e spent ~25 min on a stale-token theory; impl's one role-mapping call settled it. Action: recorded.
- LESSON: Coordinator directives asserting code facts must name the branch they are true of — a gate merged into a feature branch is not on main. The brief-fact verification rule extends to every mid-mission directive.
  Evidence: coordinator directed an org_match assertion unreachable on main; e2e refused the vacuous test and built a tripwire instead. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — brief template preamble: every code-fact claim and every later coordinator directive names the branch it is true of.
- LESSON: Environment state that specs depend on (IdP realm config, claim mappers) needs a preflight ASSERTION with a named remedy, not just service-up checks — hand-applied config drift masquerades as deep test failures two layers away.
  Evidence: Keycloak Phase-Two config lost on container recreate; invite 404; realm-export source also lacked it. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — validator role text: preflight-ASSERT the env invariants your specs assume, each with a named remedy.
- LESSON: Give the reviewer right-of-refutation on the FINAL REPORT itself — the coordinator is not exempt from overclaiming. This mission's report said "leak-proof harness"; the reviewer refuted the word with a mutation on the merged code (static Logger.* evasion) plus 4 more corrections, all applied before ship.
  Evidence: adversary REPORT-OK-with-5-corrections, 18:15. Action: APPLIED 2026-07-28 — SKILL.md Step 6: the reviewer gets right-of-refutation on the final report before it ships.

## 2026-07-31 — hcc-2440-authn (PerforceCTO/p4c-control-plane + p4c-k8s, 4 teammates incl. adversary, 3 PRs open CI-green under prs-user-merge, central claim upgraded synthetic→live, 9 self-caught scope errors)

Security hardening from a pre-written plan (revision 3, already self-corrected twice). Stacked PRs via `gh stack`. The mission's dominant finding is one error SHAPE, and the best retro material came from teammates attacking the coordinator.

- LESSON: **One shape dominated — the scope of the verifying command was narrower than the domain of the claim it supported.** 9 instances across every role, including 2 in the PLAN (which propagated plan → brief → published PR body) and 1 by the coordinator. Axes seen: principal-class, set-membership, time/truncation (x2), path/extension, property-count, revision-range, case-enumeration. Rule: state the domain BEFORE running the command, then show the command's scope equals it — name the quantifier, name what it ranges over (files? file TYPES? commits? principals? principal CLASSES?), and paste a positive control proving the command can find a known member. If scope < domain, narrow the claim in the same sentence, not a footnote. Applies at PLAN-AUTHORING time, not only verification.
  Evidence: report-input-adversary.md §1. Action: brief template + SKILL.md recon.
- LESSON: **The sharper sub-mechanism is a SHARED NOUN.** In 5 of the 9 the same word named the evidence and the claim, making the gap invisible: "realm" (local vs dev), "Maintainer" (CP role vs another app's identically-named role), "references", "the guard" (function vs deployed behaviour). Remedy: write what the load-bearing noun refers to in the EVIDENCE and in the CLAIM as two separate strings; if they differ, narrow to the evidence string. Catches the cases "state the domain" misses, because those need the REFERENT named, not the quantifier.
  Evidence: adversary retro (5). Action: brief template.
- LESSON: **Target cheaper retraction, not fewer claims.** Driving a reviewer's retraction rate to zero destroys its value — the mission's highest-value finding was published BEFORE the code existed. Compare two claims of identical certainty: an unscoped absolute cost a full retraction plus a severity downgrade plus unwinding a coordinator directive; one labelled PLAUSIBLE with the decisive experiment named cost nothing. The difference was the LABEL AT PUBLICATION. So: every finding carries (a) CONFIRMED|PLAUSIBLE, (b) its scope string, (c) the decisive experiment that would settle it. Such findings get NARROWED, not withdrawn. "Self-retraction is not the cost of being the adversary — publishing unscoped is."
  Evidence: adversary retro (5), F7 vs F10 vs F4. Action: brief template (adversary + validator roles).
- LESSON: **Brief the INVARIANT, never the assertion MECHANISM.** A brief that named the assertion shape pushed the implementer toward a primitive that turned out to be VACUOUSLY TRUE in that test framework; two of three asserts in a coordinator-APPROVED design were decorative. State what must fail and when, and leave the mechanism to be discovered under mutation.
  Evidence: k8s-impl retro (1); notMatchRegexRaw nil-raw-field. Action: brief template + SKILL.md Step 5.
- LESSON: **A coordinator-approved design can contain a vacuous assert, because reviewers check SEMANTICS and skip MECHANISM.** The design review correctly caught the logic (conditional vs unconditional) and never asked whether the assert primitives execute. Add one checklist question to every design sign-off: **"has each assertion in this test been observed to fail?"**
  Evidence: 130/130 green with the guarded string literally present in the render. Action: SKILL.md Step 5.
- LESSON: **Handed-down [VERIFIED] facts must carry their VERIFICATION SCOPE, not just their truth value** — a true statement about one subtree invited a teammate to treat that subtree as the whole world, producing a scope error downstream.
  Evidence: k8s-impl retro (2). Action: brief template.
- LESSON: **Forwarded findings need a PROVENANCE MARKER (measured-by-me vs relayed).** A relayed inference that was false in both halves reached a teammate WITH a directive to act; the instruction to act arrives with the same authority either way. Had it complied without re-deriving, it would have shipped a correct change with a wrong rationale and never triggered the escape hatch.
  Evidence: k8s-impl retro (3), F10. Action: protocols.md (coordinator messages).
- LESSON: **A calibration sample must be labelled HYPOTHESIS TO TEST, never a known instance, and the brief must pre-authorise refutation in those words.** Handing a reviewer a "known vacuous guard" that is not one is an ANCHORING attack: confirming it would have licensed DELETING A WORKING TEST. Only the words "confirm OR REFUTE it yourself" made refutation non-insubordinate.
  Evidence: adversary retro (3); F1 refuted the sample on pass one. Action: brief template (adversary role).
- LESSON: **The head-moved-void rule must ask for the RANGE, not the commit.** Both false "docs-only" claims came from single-commit scoping (`git show --name-only <head>`) against a MULTI-commit move. Require `a..b` before asking which files changed.
  Evidence: two instances, one caught by the re-gate that earned the whole gate. Action: protocols.md + merge/gate checklist.
- LESSON: **The reviewer needs right-of-refutation over the VALIDATOR's evidence claims specifically, not just over code and the report.** The validator's worst error was a REASONING error (all facts in hand, wrong conclusion, stated confidently in the section about being honest re weak evidence) — "no self-cross-check would have caught it, because both of my signals agreed and both were wrong." Only an adversarial reader with the same facts catches that class. Here it converted the mission's central claim from synthetic to LIVE, after the "final" verdict was written.
  Evidence: validator retraction of the verified-vs-synthetic split; the mintable-token correction. Action: SKILL.md Steps 3+6.
- LESSON: **Keep the WRITE regime and the SHARED-RESOURCE regime separate.** Review-only/no-git-write cost nothing and twice produced a better outcome than write access (the reviewer's own proposed remedy was worse than the author's, and right-of-reply only has teeth when the reviewer CANNOT act unilaterally). The one real cost — the reviewer could not run the decisive live probe — was the shared-resource rule, not the write rule. Conflating them would wrongly argue for giving the adversary write access.
  Evidence: adversary retro (1). Action: recorded; SKILL.md Step 3 wording.
- LESSON: **Two adjacent shapes must NOT be folded into the scope rule — different remedies.** (i) *Unverified precondition*: a `perl -pi -e` whose pattern did not match was a silent no-op, so a suite returned `ok` against UNMUTATED code — remedy: every mutation asserts its anchor landed (tools that error on a non-matching anchor avoid this structurally). (ii) *Inference from an insufficient signal*: reading `exit=1` as "the check caught it" when it was a missing-binary crash — remedy: read the artifact, not the exit code.
  Evidence: cp-impl M8; adversary's own exit-code misread. Action: brief template.
- LESSON: **Coordinator concession is load-bearing apparatus, not politeness.** Three of the reviewer's most useful findings exist because the coordinator demanded to be attacked rather than ratified, then conceded on artifacts (a severity ruling, the verified-vs-synthetic split, and a count the coordinator had proposed). Name the avoided failure mode: coordinator fiat.
  Evidence: adversary retro closing section. Action: recorded.
- LESSON: **`msg-teammate.sh` parses `--inbox` only as argv[1]; passing it AFTER the teammate name silently sends the literal string `--inbox` as the message and exits 0.** Four consecutive coordinator directives were "delivered" as that two-word string. Caught only because a teammate reported "no directives received", contradicting four rc=0 results. This is the exact failure class the script exists to prevent, in a new shape: not a swallowed Enter, but a corrupted payload reported as success.
  Evidence: journal 16:58. Action: **script fix (proposed) — refuse a message beginning with `--`, or accept `--inbox` positionally anywhere**; plus coordinator rule: a teammate reporting "no directive received" OUTRANKS a prior rc=0.
- LESSON: **Create every teammate's inbox file at spawn.** A standing "poll your inbox between steps" instruction pointing at a nonexistent file is dead weight on every step; one teammate's inbox never existed at all while it was told to poll it.
  Evidence: adversary retro (4). Action: **script fix (proposed) — spawn-teammate.sh touches `inbox-<name>.md`**.
- LESSON: **Do not tell a teammate something is outstanding without reading its status file** — reporting a gate as still-awaited 20 minutes after it was posted inverts the point of the status protocol: if the coordinator is not reading the file, the file is not the channel.
  Evidence: adversary retro (4). Action: recorded.
- LESSON: **"Be shorter" is the WRONG lesson from long coordinator messages; REDUNDANCY is the problem.** ~20-30% of message volume restated the teammate's own findings back to it before the new ask. The three longest messages were the highest-value ones. Lead with the directive; reference prior findings by ID.
  Evidence: adversary retro (4); k8s-impl retro (praise passages lowest-information-per-token). Action: protocols.md.
- LESSON: **Never assert a teammate's evidence for it.** The coordinator told a teammate which gates it had run green; it had not run two of them, and would have inherited an unearned citation.
  Evidence: k8s-impl retro (4). Action: recorded.
- LESSON: **Git worktrees do not inherit untracked files, so `.envrc`/direnv silently fails** and the devshell's tools are off PATH; and entering the devshell can rewrite the MAIN checkout's hooks because worktrees share `.git`. Brief the exact devshell invocation (`nix develop -c ...`) and warn about the shared git dir.
  Evidence: cp-impl created its own `.envrc`; k8s-impl and validator both burned round-trips; two hook-rewrite disclosures. Action: brief template + SKILL.md Step 2.
- LESSON: **Command output may be proxied or summarised by the environment.** A wrapper rewrote an `rg` invocation and produced mutually inconsistent check counts from the same PR. Any state worth reporting must be re-queried structurally (`--json`), never read from a human-formatted summary.
  Evidence: k8s-impl retro (2), two instances. Action: brief template.
- LESSON: **The report-refutation gate earns its cost on the coordinator's own report, every time.** This mission's draft was REPORT-BLOCKED with four corrections, the worst of which killed its headline sentence: it claimed the dominant error shape appeared "across every role" while the table omitted one of the five sessions, making the strongest claim the least true one. The reviewer fixed it by ADDING the missing instance from that session's own verdict rather than softening the sentence — so the count ended up both correct and larger. It also caught an unqualified absolute ("every guard the reviewer attacked was real", falsified by two of its own findings), a PLAUSIBLE-rated fact stated flatly, and a paraphrase that SOFTENED a teammate's harsher self-caveat. Corollary: re-verify in the FILE, not from the coordinator's summary of its own edits, and check for absolutes newly INTRODUCED while editing.
  Evidence: adversary REPORT-BLOCKED -> 4 corrections -> REPORT-OK, 2026-07-31. Action: SKILL.md Step 6 (already required; this is the second mission where it caught coordinator overclaiming — keep).
- LESSON: **Let the reviewer refuse a widening of its own definition, including one the coordinator offers in the coordinator's disfavour.** Applying the corrections, the coordinator found a further defect of its own (an unsourced chart) and offered it as a thirteenth instance of the dominant shape. The reviewer REFUSED it, on the principled line that every counted instance has a *verifying command* whose scope can be compared against a claim domain, and an unsourced number has none — explicitly noting that it had rejected a teammate's error on the same grounds, so admitting the coordinator's would make it "inconsistent in your favour, which is the one direction I must not err." A category that grows to absorb every nearby mistake stops being a finding. Note the healthy shape of the exchange: the coordinator volunteered evidence against itself and the reviewer declined to over-credit it.
  Evidence: ruling on the removed mutations-per-artifact chart, 2026-07-31. Action: brief template (adversary role) — defending the definition is part of the job.
- LESSON: **The multiplexer CLI can change under a running mission.** `msg-teammate.sh` called `herdr agent prompt` (per a comment recording a proto17 rename of `agent send`) and `agent wait --until`; the installed build had `agent send` and `wait --status`. Sends succeeded for hours, then every one printed herdr's help and exited 2. Two things made it recoverable: `--inbox` mirrors the message to disk BEFORE attempting delivery (all four stranded directives were intact), and the script exits nonzero on unconfirmed delivery.
