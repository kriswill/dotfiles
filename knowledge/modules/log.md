  as k (`createUser = false`, member-less `hermes` group) instead of the
  module's `hermes` user. Hermes is single-owner, and the shared-group cut
  failed with `storage_unavailable`
  ([decision](../decisions/hermes-agent-system-service.md)).

- **Creation**: [hermes-agent](hermes-agent.md) re-exports the upstream
  NousResearch/hermes-agent NixOS module (`services.hermes-agent`), tag-pinned
  to `v0.22.5`, without a nixpkgs follow, and with its home-manager input cut.
  [nebula-hermes-agent](nebula-hermes-agent.md) enables it on nebula in native
